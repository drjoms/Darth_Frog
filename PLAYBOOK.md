🔴 SCENARIO 1: The "Patent Troll" Strike (Reactive Defense)
Target Profile: External Patent Assertion Entities (PAEs) looking for "gaps" in un-documented technical implementations.
    • The Threat: A predator identifies a specific component in your new product and files a patent, claiming it is a "novel invention." They then demand high-royalty settlements or threaten litigation. 
    • The Darth Frog Response: Perform a Minimum Viable Disclosure (MVD). Release the "Functional Path" of that component, supported by the Vault's timestamped proof that the component was a logical necessity of your system long before the troll's filing. 
    • The Tactical Result: The troll's patent is invalidated under 35 U.S.C. § 103 (Obviousness). 
    • Business Impact: Zero royalty payments. Litigation costs minimized. Patent threat neutralized. 
🟢 SCENARIO 2: The "Pre-emptive Landmine" (Proactive Deterrence)
Target Profile: Competitors attempting to "patent-wrap" or enclose emerging technical standards.
    • The Threat: A competitor is attempting to build a "patent wall" around a new industry standard to force all other players to pay them for use. 
    • The Darth Frog Response: Implement Strategic Seeding. Before the competitor can file, the company releases a "Seed" (a technical whitepaper or open-source implementation) describing the Functional Necessity of the core components. 
    • The Tactical Result: The "Seed" becomes Public Prior Art. Any attempt by the competitor to patent those components is met with an immediate "Obviousness" invalidation. 
    • Business Impact: Market freedom secured. Competitor's "patent wall" is rendered useless before it is even built. 
🔴 SCENARIO 3: The "Disgruntled Mole" (Internal Misappropriation)
Target Profile: Internal employees or contractors attempting to steal IP for sale to competitors or foreign entities.
    • The Threat: A high-level engineer steals blueprints and attempts to sell them to a third party.

🟣 SCENARIO 4: The "Aikido Move" (The Derivative Theft Capture)
Target Profile: A competitor who successfully steals IP and attempts to "claim" it via their own patent filing.
    • The Threat: A competitor steals your blueprints, builds a device, and files a patent on it, thinking they have "legalized" the stolen technology. 
    • The Darth Frog Response: Initiate a Derivation Proceeding combined with an MVD. Use the Vault to prove original ownership and use the competitor's own public filing as Prior Art against them.

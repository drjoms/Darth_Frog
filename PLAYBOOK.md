🔴 SCENARIO 1: The "Patent Troll" Strike (Reactive Defense)

    Target Profile: External Patent Assertion Entities (PAEs) looking for "gaps" in undocumented technical implementations.
    The Threat: A predator identifies a specific component in your product and files a patent, claiming it is a "novel invention." They then demand high-royalty settlements or threaten litigation.
    The Darth Frog Response:
        1. Assessment & Signaling: Upon identifying a threat (via a patent or a public pre-patent filing), assess the risk with legal counsel. Provide the opposing party with cryptographic evidence of "prior designs" and signal the existence of the Darth Frog (DF) doctrine and protocol (including the link to the GitHub repository).
        2. Escalated Defense (MVD): If the opposing party continues to apply pressure, perform a Minimum Viable Disclosure (MVD) to establish a baseline defense. Release the "Functional Path" of that component, supported by the Vault's timestamped proof that the component was a logical necessity of your system long before the troll's filing. This provides cryptographic evidence that the design was documented. Should the matter reach court, this significantly increases the probability of winning on the grounds of obviousness.
    The Tactical Result: The troll's patent is invalidated under 35 U.S.C. § 103 (Obviousness).
    Alternative Tactical Result: The patent predator decides not to bite.
    Business Impact: Zero royalty payments. Litigation costs minimized. Potential threat of the offending patent is neutralized.

🔴 SCENARIO 2: The "Disgruntled Mole" (Internal Misappropriation)

    Target Profile: Internal employees or contractors attempting to steal IP for sale to competitors or foreign entities.
    The Threat: A high-level engineer steals digital blueprints and sells them to a third party.
    The Escalated Threat: The third party attempts to "legalize" the theft by proactively filing patents on the stolen IP, or by using the blueprints to rapidly iterate on designs to launch competing products.
    The Darth Frog Response:
        Detection Phase: Monitor patent filings for suspicious "coincidental" innovations or identify the market presence of cloned components (e.g., noticing your proprietary parts appearing in a competitor's product).
        The Tripwire: Once a threat is identified, use the Vault's Forensic Provenance to correlate the offending party's patent claims or product designs against your timestamped Functional Paths.
        The Leverage: Present the unforgeable, cryptographic "Chain of Custody" to the offending party. Use the correlation to demonstrate the statistical improbability of independent convergence—showing that the degree of coincidence between their design and your specific, timestamped functional properties (tolerances, proportions, and sequences) renders the "independent development" defense untenable.
    The Tactical Result: The theft is no longer a "he-said/she-said" dispute; it is a demonstrated misappropriation of proprietary data. The competitor is forced to choose between high-stakes litigation or an immediate strategic settlement.
    Business Impact:
        Strategic Settlement: High-leverage extraction of royalties, licensing fees, or exit settlements.
        Prosecution Support: High-confidence evidence for streamlined criminal/civil action (e.g., theft of trade secrets).
        Market Protection: Aggressive neutralization of unfair competitors and predatory players.
